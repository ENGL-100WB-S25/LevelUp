**Career Portfolio**  
Up to 2,000 XP, due May 14

As the capstone project for this course, you will create a comprehensive professional portfolio that showcases the communication skills you've developed throughout the semester. This portfolio should demonstrate your ability to adapt professional writing across platforms while maintaining a consistent personal brand. More than just a collection of documents, this portfolio should tell a compelling story about your professional identity and readiness for your chosen career path.

# **Learning Objectives:**

* Develop a cohesive professional brand  
* Adapt writing for different professional platforms  
* Create portfolio-worthy professional documents  
* Demonstrate platform-specific writing skills  
* Build a foundation for ongoing professional development

This assignment supports the following Course Learning Outcomes:

1\. Apply rhetorical principles to analyze and adapt communication for diverse business audiences, purposes, and contexts.  
2\. Produce professional-quality documents in various business genres, demonstrating mastery of conventions and best practices.  
4\. Develop and maintain a professional identity across digital and traditional platforms.

---

# **Portfolio Components:**

1. Professional Biography Package   
* Long-form narrative version (250-300 words)  
* LinkedIn summary version (150-200 words)  
* Short elevator pitch version (50-75 words)  
* Social media micro-versions (for X, Bluesky, Instagram, etc.)  
2. Career Documents Package  
* Tailored resume for specific role  
* Cover letter  
* Post-interview “thank you” email  
3. Content Showcase (choose 3\)  
* White paper executive summary \+ excerpt  
* Project proposal excerpt  
* Crisis communication samples  
* Other relevant writing samples drawn from other classes in your major, alongside work from this class:  
  * Technical documentation  
  * Industry analysis  
  * Professional blog post  


# **Format Requirements:**

* Professional design throughout  
* Consistent personal branding  
* Clear navigation/organization  
* Portfolio site or PDF compilation
